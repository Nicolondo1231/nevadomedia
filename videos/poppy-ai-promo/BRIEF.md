## Intent

A direct-response social ad for Poppy AI, a visual AI workspace for content
creators. Sold to creators and marketers who have tried ChatGPT for content and
got generic output. The angle is the mechanism: Poppy studies the videos that
are already outperforming, and studies the creator's own videos for voice, so
what comes out sounds like them instead of like the internet's average.

Tone is conspiratorial and punchy — the hook literally frames it as something
that "feels illegal to know about". Sound-off-first: high-retention short-form
pacing, hard cuts on the beat, big burned-in captions, no slow fades.

## Assets

None supplied. All UI on screen is invented for this ad (see Notes).

## Customizations

- VO_MODE: verbatim. The user's script copy is final and must be spoken and
  captioned word for word, not restructured.
- No-capture mode: do not crawl or scrape getpoppy.ai. The user explicitly
  chose generated, stylized Poppy-style UI over real product screens.
- Three numbered steps in the value beat must read as 1 / 2 / 3 on screen.
- Count-up animation on the follower stat: 900 -> 97,000.
- Hold every on-screen number at least 0.4s so it stays readable.

## Notes

- Brand colors are fixed: #7F78EB lavender (primary), #F4938F salmon (accent).
  Suggested neutrals: #12101F near-black, #F7F6FD off-white.
- All "Poppy UI" is a STYLIZED interpretation, not a replica of the real
  product. Keep UI labels generic ("Paste a link", "Analyzing...", "Outlier",
  "Your voice"). Placeholder thumbnails must be abstract colored rectangles.
- Do not render any real third-party logo, real YouTube page, real channel
  name, or any recognizable real person's face. Avatar tiles in the social
  proof grid must be abstract and non-identifiable.
- The performance claims in the script ($29K on $800 spend, 36X ROAS, 14X
  outliers, 13,000+ creators, 4.9/5, 900 -> ~100,000 followers) are the
  advertiser's own copy, carried verbatim from the supplied script.
- Cut on the beat: hard cuts only, scale-punches and whip-pans, staggered
  entrances, text punches in with slight overshoot.
